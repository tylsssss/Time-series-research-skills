# Pattern H — Replace a Coarse Proxy with an Operational Framework

## Route When

Use when a qualitative label, binary hierarchy, aggregate metric, or sufficiency statement cannot express a comparison required by the task.

```text
coarse proxy P groups distinct behaviors
-> task requires distinction D
-> define operational quantity Q(D)
-> characterize subjects under Q
-> connect the distinction to observable or constructive cases
```

## Structural Contract

- Exhibit a counterexample or unresolved comparison under the old proxy.
- Define the new quantity on precise objects and assumptions.
- Establish the minimum required property such as measurability, consistency, a bound, or necessity.
- Distinguish at least two relevant mechanisms or regimes.
- Connect formal distinctions to an empirical or constructive counterpart without equating them.

## Minimum Contrast

Construct a case the old proxy ties or misranks, evaluate the new quantity, verify one formal property, test the predicted ordering or boundary, and state what the framework cannot imply about downstream performance.

## Reject or Return When

Reject or bound the framework when the old proxy already distinguishes the subjects, the new quantity is unidentifiable or intractable, assumptions exclude the target, controlled ordering fails, or no model, experiment, or scientific conclusion changes.

## Time-Series Hazards

Define the mathematical object, sampling process, scope, and data-generating assumptions before claiming expressivity, recoverability, robustness, ranking reliability, or identifiability distinctions.
