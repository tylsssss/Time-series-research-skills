# Personal Pattern Index

This is the shipped `default` profile: **no precedent cards are configured.**

Use this index only after recording the independent candidate set at Stage 5. Personal
cards are conditional precedents for adversarial testing, not evidence, default
solutions, or candidate generators.

## Routing Rules

1. Compare the frozen candidate's failure location, causal link, invariant, and broken
   assumptions with the signatures below.
2. Load at most one card per research candidate.
3. Require at least one structural mismatch or boundary before retaining an analogy.
4. Load no card when only terminology, module type, diagram shape, or application area
   matches.
5. Use a card first to generate rivals, risks, and falsifiers; use it to refine the
   mechanism only when the causal structure matches.
6. Cite project evidence and literature independently; never cite a personal card as
   external evidence.

## Writing your own rows

A useful row has three parts: a **structural signature** (a shape of failure and
mechanism, not a method family), the **card** that tests it, and an **exclusion** — the
boundary at which the analogy stops being informative. If you cannot state the
exclusion, the row is a horoscope: it will "match" everything and constrain nothing.

The three shipped card slots are `personal-pattern-card-a.md`,
`personal-pattern-card-b.md`, and `personal-pattern-card-c.md`. Their
filenames are fixed by the controller's router; rewrite their contents rather than
renaming them, and add a fourth card by adding a row here.
